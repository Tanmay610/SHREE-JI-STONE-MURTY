Years of Experience, Trusted Craftsmanship

At Shreeji Stone murty, we have been working in the field of stone craftsmanship and statue manufacturing since 2005. With years of experience, our aim is to combine beautiful designs, excellent quality, and traditional craftsmanship with modern requirements.

We specialize in creating marble statues of Hindu deities, along with attractive fountains, tables, sofas, and chhatris (canopies). We also manufacture a wide range of designs and artworks using Sandstone, Pink Stone, and Red Stone.

Our team of experienced craftsmen carefully creates every statue and stone product with precision and dedication. We give special attention to fulfilling customer requirements, preferences, and customized designs.

"Our objective is not simply to shape stone, but to create artworks that remain a lasting attraction through their beauty, craftsmanship, and quality."

Premium Materials

We work with the finest natural stones to create our masterpieces. Each material is carefully selected to suit the specific artwork, whether it is a delicate deity statue or a robust outdoor fountain.

  • Marble: Renowned for its purity and smooth finish, perfect for divine statues.
  • Sandstone: Offers a warm, textured appeal ideal for architectural carvings.
  • Pink Stone: Brings a unique, elegant hue to traditional temples and chhatris.
  • Red Stone: Known for its strength and vibrant presence in structural artworks.
